What it is

corporate CULTure AT large.

HawThorne is a preset, a prompt pack, for SillyTavern. Instead of one voice writing everything, a Director writes each turn in their own genre style.

You choose which Directors are on shift. Each turn, one of them is picked at random.

Under the hood

no extension. just macros.

The rotation runs entirely in SillyTavern's own macro language, re-evaluated on every generation. No scripts, no extension to install.

  1. Reset. The init block runs first. It saves last turn's Director as the predecessor and sets a counter to zero.
  2. Roll. Every Director you switched on runs the same three lines: add one to the counter, roll a die with that many sides, and take the booth on a 1.
  3. Why it's fair. The k-th Director wins its own roll with a 1-in-k chance, then has to survive every roll after it. That multiplies out to exactly 1-in-N for each of N Directors, in one pass, without the preset ever knowing N ahead of time. It's reservoir sampling.
  4. Hand-off. The winner's rules, banned words and voice load. A framing (Collaborative, Adversarial, Hostile Takeover, Auditor, Ghost) decides how it treats its predecessor, and one turn in four the predecessor leaves a note on its monitor. Same Director twice in a row? It's told to prove its range.
